## 💡 My Approach

I believe the future of technology lies in combining **intelligent automation** with **strong data foundations** and **practical business understanding**.

1. **Understand the operation** — map the real workflow before automating anything.
2. **Ground in data** — analytics first, so automation acts on truth, not guesses.
3. **Build agents that act** — not dashboards that sit idle.
4. **Measure the value** — efficiency, accuracy, and business outcomes.

<details>
<summary><b>🔍 How I typically engage a problem</b></summary>
<br>

- Audit the workflow → find the bottlenecks and repetition.
- Design the data layer → sources, models, and quality checks.
- Prototype the agent/automation → n8n + LLM + APIs.
- Harden it → error handling, logging, human-in-the-loop where it matters.
- Ship & iterate → measure, refine, scale.

</details>
